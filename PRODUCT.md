# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Cashiers working in a fast-paced environment. The primary job to be done is to quickly and accurately take customer orders and process payments.

## Product Purpose
A robust, fast, and premium Restaurant POS System. Success looks like minimizing the time it takes a cashier to complete a transaction while providing an interface that feels modern and reliable.

## Brand Personality
Premium, fast, friendly.

## Anti-references
Avoid "AI slop" (clichéd layouts, overused decorative elements like gradient text, generic floating ghost-cards, side-stripe borders). Avoid old, outdated POS designs that look like traditional enterprise software.

## Design Principles
- **Efficiency over decoration**: Cashiers need to process orders quickly. Interactions must be fast and intuitive.
- **Premium simplicity**: Use generous whitespace, crisp typography, and deliberate accent colors rather than heavy boxes and generic shadows.
- **Unmistakable feedback**: Actions (like adding to cart or processing payment) must have immediate, clear visual feedback to prevent double-entry errors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Large touch targets**: Ensure all interactive elements (buttons, menu items) meet or exceed the recommended minimums for fast-paced POS environments (at least 48x48px, ideally larger for primary actions).
- **High contrast**: Ensure text is easily legible at a glance.
- **Clear state indication**: Selected items and disabled actions must be immediately obvious without relying solely on color.
